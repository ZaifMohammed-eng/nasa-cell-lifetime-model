Li-ion cell end-of-life forecaster: live demo

Forecasts the cycle at which an 18650 cell falls to 80 % of its initial capacity from its first cycles of NASA aging data, with a 90 % interval. Inputs outside the training envelope are routed to an engineer instead of answered. Pick a case, or send your own request.
